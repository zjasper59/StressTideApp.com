About StressTide

StressTide was created to help users understand their workload, organize their tasks, and manage stress before it builds up.

Our Mission

StressTide aims to make stress easier to understand. Instead of only tracking tasks, the app helps users see how deadlines, difficulty, and daily workload patterns can affect their stress levels.

Why StressTide Was Created

Life can feel overwhelming when tasks pile up without warning. StressTide was built to give users a clearer picture of what they need to do, how difficult their workload may be, and when stress might start to increase.

What StressTide Helps With

Organization

Keep tasks, subjects, deadlines, and difficulty levels in one place.

Awareness

Understand how your workload changes across different days.

Planning

Make smarter decisions before deadlines start stacking up.
